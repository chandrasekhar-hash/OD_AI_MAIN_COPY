OUR MISSION & VISION

Empowering Odisha Through Artificial Intelligence

OD AI HUB is an end-to-end AI talent-to-innovation platform empowering students, professionals, institutions, and businesses to Learn, Build, Innovate, and Scale. We believe that talent in Tier-2 and Tier-3 cities in Odisha holds tremendous potential that, with the right industry alignment and mentorship, can compete globally.

Rooted in Berhampur, the cultural and educational capital of Southern Odisha, we are bridging the gap between classroom theory and real-world deployment. From school children exploring their first line of Python in Class 5, to college graduates building neural networks, to local enterprises automating workflows with custom LLMs — OD AI HUB is the catalyst for technological empowerment.

01 • ACADEMY

OD AI Academy

Curricula aligned with global tech leaders. 6 specialized learning paths spanning school students, diploma holders, college graduates, and working professionals with 100% hands-on project work.

02 • TECHNOLOGIES

OD AI Technologies

03 • INNOVATION LAB

OD AI Innovation Lab

Rapid prototyping facilities in Berhampur for AgriTech soil telemetry, IoT sensor networks, clinical health diagnostics, and civic robotics built by local talent.

04 • STARTUP INCUBATOR

OD AI Startups

From idea to venture capital. Pre-seed incubation, mentorship, compute grants, and institutional funding access for founders solving regional and national challenges.
